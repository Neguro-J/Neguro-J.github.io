The answer is simple: I wanted a space to show, step by step, this stage of my life as a person — as Neguro — before anything else, not just as a researcher.

I don't want this to be purely an academic logbook. I want it to reflect someone who's passionate about inventing, extending, testing, and building things. So not everything here is going to be about robotics. Sometimes it'll be some weird simulation I saw online and wanted to try in MATLAB. Did it work? Why not? Did something explode? What did I do to fix it? That process — the trial, the error, the curiosity — is just as much part of the story as the final result.

I'm passionate about technology and about everything that can be built with it, and I want this space to reflect exactly that. I firmly believe you have to stay creative and keep learning, not just for yourself, but so you can teach others to be just as creative, to invent, to dare. We need to encourage a love for knowledge.

So welcome to this little corner of the internet. There will be robots, sure, but there will also be random experiments, half-baked ideas, and probably more than one "I have no idea why this isn't working yet." And that's fine — that's how you learn.
